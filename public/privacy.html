Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Demo Clinic ("we", "us") handles information when you book an appointment on our website or chat with us on WhatsApp.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Your WhatsApp messages to us and our replies.
  • Appointment details: service, doctor, date, time and status.

Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and book, confirm or remind you about appointments.
  • To pass your chat to our staff when you ask for a person, or when we cannot help.
  • To keep a record of appointments so the clinic can run its day.

Who can see it

Only clinic staff can see your appointment records. We do not sell your information. We use these service providers to run the service: Meta (WhatsApp), OpenAI (to write chat replies), Google (spreadsheets used for our records) and our website hosting provider. They process information only to provide their service to us.

Health information

Please do not send detailed medical information in chat. Our assistant does not give medical advice or diagnosis. For an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.

How long we keep it

We keep appointment and chat records only as long as the clinic needs them for its work, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email us at the address below and we will reply within 30 days.
